The *quaternion group* is the group $Q$ generated by the complex matrices

$$
A = \begin{pmatrix}
0 & 1\\
-1 & 0\\
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B = \begin{pmatrix}
0 & i\\
i & 0\\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

$$
I=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
A=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
B=\begin{pmatrix}0&i\\ i&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
AB=\begin{pmatrix}i&0\\0&-i\end{pmatrix}.
$$
