Our paper [Mermin-Peres magic rectangles modulo odd primes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20746) appeared on arXiv today. A little arXiv drama:
[Lorenzo Ciardo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14632) and [Markus Frembs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16490) independently obtained the same result
using different techniques, with all three preprints appearing within days of each other!
