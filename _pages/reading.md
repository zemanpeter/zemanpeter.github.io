* Murphy: *$$C^*$$-Algebras and Operator Theory*
* Deitmar, Echterhoff: *Principles of Harmonic Analysis*
* Freslon: *Compact Matrix Quantum Groups and Their Combinatorics*
* Neshveyev, Tuset: *Compact Quantum Groups and Their Representation Categories*
* Rotman: *An Introduction to the Theory of Groups*

* Godsil, Royle: *Algebraic Graph Theory*
