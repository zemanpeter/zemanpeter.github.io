- Nielsen, Chuang: _Quantum Computation and Quantum Information_
- Hall: _Quantum Theory for Mathematicians_

- Conway: _A Course in Functional Analysis_
- Murphy: _$$C^_$$-Algebras and Operator Theory\*
- Deitmar, Echterhoff: _Principles of Harmonic Analysis_
- Freslon: _Compact Matrix Quantum Groups and Their Combinatorics_
- Neshveyev, Tuset: _Compact Quantum Groups and Their Representation Categories_
- Rotman: _An Introduction to the Theory of Groups_
- Schupp, Lyndon: _Combinatorial Group Theory_
- Dixon, Mortimer: _Permutation Groups_
- Etingof et al.: _Introduction to Representation Theory_
- Hall: _Lie Groups, Lie Algebras and Representations_
- Prasolov: _Elements of Combinatorial and Differential Topology_
- Rotman: _An Introduction to Algebraic Topology_
- Breadon: _Topology and Geometry_
- Farkas, Kra: _Riemann Surfaces_
- Kantor, Matoušek, Šámal: _Mathematics++_
- Godsil, Royle: _Algebraic Graph Theory_
